# Complete zombie outfits

Corrects the head display rotation from 180 degrees to 0. Adds seven custom equipment definitions with individual 256x128 humanoid and leggings textures. Artwork uses decayed olive cloth, crimson wraps, steel/cyan plate, lime hazard gear, crystalline frost armor, studded brute leather and purple/gold Grave King armor.

Requires BlinkZombie 0.3.2 for equipping the body assets. Its automatic pack updater reads latest.properties; no resource-pack configuration change is needed after the original automatic-update setup. Existing loaded custom mobs and mobs reloaded from chunks are re-equipped once using appearance version 2. Normal player armor is unchanged.

New original armor artwork generated with the built-in image tool as seven flat pixel-art breastplate panels and a material tile; standard humanoid equipment UV sheets assembled from those panels. Existing heads, keys, sword assets and normal movement animations are retained. Head rotation, equipment references and image sizes were checked; in-game fit still needs confirmation.
